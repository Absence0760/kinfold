## Backend gaps

- [ ] **Loans without a request never become `active`.** Only a request moving to `paid` activates its signed loans, so a loan created directly (future Home Fund `fund_loans`) stays `signed`, can't be forgiven or paused, and doesn't show missed installments. **Fix:** activate on disbursement from the payments webhook (a service-only `activate_loan(loan)` once the partner confirms the money moved), or from the `fund_loans` disbursement path. **When:** Stage 3, with `fund_loans`.
- [ ] **Partial refunds in the ledger.** `mark_refunded` stores `refunded_cents`, which can be less than the contribution's amount, but status just says `refunded`. **Fix:** the `payments-webhook` refund journal must post `refunded_cents`, not `amount_cents`. Add a pgTAP check once the journal-posting code exists. **When:** Stage 1, step 1f (payments).
- [ ] **A payout returned after a refund isn't blocked in the database.** `refunds_due` splits captured money across live payouts oldest first. If a payout were marked `returned` after contributions were already refunded, the split wouldn't re-run. Normal paths can't reach this, because refusing or withdrawing needs no live payout, but nothing forbids it. **Fix:** in the payout guard, refuse moving a payout to `returned`/`failed` when the request already has refunded contributions, and route it to a person instead. **When:** Stage 1, step 1f.

- [ ] **The pool pays after people today.** `run_pool_allocation` computes need as amount minus what people pledged, so gifts only save the pool money. `design.md` now says the pool pays its covered amount first and people fund the rest, which is also the order FEMA expects in declared disasters. **Fix:** compute the pool's share from the cover terms (amount up to the cap, minus the member's share), independent of pledges, with pgTAP and the web `allocation.ts` reference updated together. **When:** Stage 2, step 2c, with the cover terms in 2e.
- [ ] **No record of other money for the same damage.** Members are meant to declare other cover and any FEMA, SBA, state or charity money, and report recoveries later (`design.md`, Fraud-proofing and Legal reality). **Fix:** a `request_recoveries` table (source, amount, date, evidence), owner-written and service-verified, which the pool run and payouts subtract; with pgTAP. **When:** Stage 1, step 1c.
- [ ] **Open books need a schedule.** `publish_open_books(month)` exists but nothing calls it. **Fix:** a monthly `pg_cron` job (or the `reconcile` edge function) for the month that ended over a week ago, plus an alert if a month is missing. A payout returned after its month was published stays counted; reconcile such corrections into the next month, never by republishing. **When:** Stage 2, step 2d.
- [ ] **Fair-lending demographics aren't stored.** The CDFI report and the bias audits need race, ethnicity and sex (age may be asked but never weighted against an applicant), and the schema holds none. **Fix:** an optional table in the `private` schema, unreadable by the scorer, reviewers and panel, filled from the government monitoring form on home-purchase and refinance loans (Reg B 1002.13) or a voluntary self-test with its four disclosures (1002.5(b)(1)). Needs `/safe-migration` and compliance review. The pool's audit is an insurance-law question; it estimates race from surname and geography (BISG) instead of asking. **When:** before the first Home Fund application (Stage 3).
